Privacy Policy

TaskMatrix Mobile · Last updated: October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TaskMatrix Mobile ("the App", "we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our Android application. By installing and using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information you create in the App. TaskMatrix Mobile helps you organize tasks. The tasks, notes, categories, reminders, and settings you enter are stored on your device. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, phone number, or address.

Information collected automatically. To keep the App working, improve it, and show ads, the third-party services listed in Section 4 may automatically collect:

  • Device information (device model, operating system version, language, screen size)
  • Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID) and app instance identifiers
  • IP address and approximate region (derived from IP, not precise location)
  • App usage and interaction data (screens viewed, features used)
  • Crash logs and performance diagnostics

Information you send us. If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the content of your message, which we use only to reply to you.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, SMS, call logs, or financial information.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide and maintain the features of the App
  • To send task reminders and notifications that you enable
  • To analyze usage and fix bugs and crashes
  • To improve and develop new features
  • To display advertising and measure its performance
  • To respond to your support requests

We do not sell your personal information.
